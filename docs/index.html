Google Health Sync

An Obsidian plugin that puts daily calories eaten and daily calories burned side by side, in both Google Health and your Obsidian vault.

  • Sends each day's calories, carbs, fat and protein from your food log note to Google Health as one "Daily intake" entry.
  • Writes Google Health's total calories burned back into the same note.

It runs entirely inside Obsidian on your own device and talks directly to Google. There is no server in between.
