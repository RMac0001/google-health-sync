Privacy policy: Google Health Sync

Last updated: September 27, 2026

What the plugin does

Google Health Sync is an Obsidian plugin. It reads daily nutrition totals from notes in your own Obsidian vault and writes them to your Google Health account, and it reads your daily calories burned from Google Health and writes them to those notes.

Data it accesses

  • From your vault: the calorie, carbohydrate, fat and protein properties of your daily food log notes.
  • From Google Health, with your permission: your nutrition entries (to find and replace the plugin's own "Daily intake" entries) and your daily total calories burned.

Where data goes

  • The plugin runs only on your device, inside Obsidian. It sends data directly between your device and Google's APIs. There is no server operated by the plugin, and no data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone else.
  • Your Google sign-in (refresh token) and OAuth client secret are stored in Obsidian's secret storage on each device, not in your notes.
  • The sign-in page on this site only displays the one-time sign-in code in your browser so you can copy it. It does not send, store or log the code. Like any website, this site is hosted by GitHub, which may keep standard server logs.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Health data is used only to provide the sync described above, is not used for advertising, and is not transferred to anyone.

Removing access

Disconnect in the plugin's settings, and revoke access at any time in your Google account under Security → Third-party connections. Entries the plugin created stay in Google Health until you delete them there.
